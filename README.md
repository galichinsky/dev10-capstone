# Quality of Life vs Happiness: Data Analysis and Visualization

## Overview
This project explores the relationship between quality of life and happiness across countries using data from global reports. The analysis identifies key factors influencing quality of life and happiness, highlights regional trends, and uncovers outliers. An interactive web application built with Dash and Plotly provides visual insights into the findings.

---

## Features
- **Interactive Visualizations**:
  - Scatter plots, tables, bar charts, a correlation matrix and trendlines to explore relationships between metrics.
  - Regional comparisons of quality of life and happiness scores.
- **Outlier Detection**:
  - Identifies countries that perform significantly better or worse than expected.
- **Statistical Analysis**:
  - Correlation and regression model to quantify relationships between variables.

---

## Datasets
- **Quality of Life Data**:
  - Metrics: Stability, rights, health, safety, climate, costs, popularity.
  - Source: [WorldData.info](https://www.worlddata.info/quality-of-life.php#google_vignette)
- **Happiness Data**:
  - Metrics: Ladder scores, GDP, social support, healthy life expectancy, freedom, generosity and perceptions of corruption.
  - Source: [World Happiness Report](https://worldhappiness.report/)
